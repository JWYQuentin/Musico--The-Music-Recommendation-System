# Popularity bias and genre fairness

Generated by `python -m m4a_rec.bias report`. Do not edit by hand; rerun instead.

Measured on `test`, on each user's top 10 once already-played tracks are dropped.
Every model is fitted on both train splits. A discovery is a track a user played in the
period for the first time.

## One fix: a popularity penalty

`tt_id + ranker_artist` with each candidate's score reduced by `lambda` = 0.1 times its popularity, both
standardised within the user's list. `lambda` was chosen on val (`reports/bias_tuning.md`).
